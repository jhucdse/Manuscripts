This repository includes codes for a paper that used Medicare Fee For Services (FFS) Claims.
	1	The folder titled "2026_J-Diabetes-Metab-Disord_Wang" contains SAS codes written by Yingqi Wang (ScM, 2025) for an analysis of type 2 diabetes treatment patterns among individuals with and without Alzheimer's disease.
The analysis was published in Journal of Diabetes and Metabolic Disorders in 2025 (PubMed link: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41510365/).
Full Citation: Wang Y, Alexander GC, Mehta HB. Treatment of type 2 diabetes among medicare beneficiaries with and without alzheimer's disease: A retrospective cohort study. J Diabetes Metab Disord. 2026 Jan 6;25(1):25. doi: 10.1007/s40200-025-01838-8. PMID: 41510365; PMCID: PMC12775190.
